Privacy Policy

GB Books is an internal application used only by GB Inspections (San Diego, CA) to access its own QuickBooks Online data. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from outside users.

Data accessed through QuickBooks Online is used only for GB Inspections' internal bookkeeping. GB Inspections does not sell, rent, or share this data with any third party. Access tokens are stored securely and can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the app in QuickBooks.
